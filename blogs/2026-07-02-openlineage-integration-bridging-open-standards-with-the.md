Key Takeaways Connect any OpenLineage-compatible orchestrator to the Precisely Data Integrity Suite in minutes — no custom connector required. Dataset-level and column-level lineage are both captured automatically based on the event payload. Lineage is always complete: when a dataset hasn’t been formally discovered yet, the catalog creates placeholders and automatically enriches them when discovery runs.
